# NNNN. Title in the imperative

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](./NNNN-title.md)
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Constraints, deadlines, what was already known, what
the alternatives were. Written so someone who was not there can follow it.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes easy, and what it rules out. Include the drawbacks honestly.
A record with no downsides listed is a record nobody trusted enough to write properly.
